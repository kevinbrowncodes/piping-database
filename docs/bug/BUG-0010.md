# BUG-0010 — Calculator backspace after "feet" wipes the entry

> Status: Resolved · Severity: Low · Found: device QA 2026-09-03

## Summary

Typing **5**, **feet**, then **⌫** clears the whole entry to 0'-0" instead
of undoing the feet keystroke (back to "5").

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Calculator → AC → 5 → feet → ⌫.

## Expected vs Actual

- **Expected:** display returns to 0'-5" (the digits "5" are back in the
  inch field, i.e. the feet key is undone).
- **Actual:** 0'-0" — the five is gone.

## Root Cause

`TradeCalcEngine.backspace()` sets `feetPart = nil` when there are no digits
and no fraction, discarding the number that `feet()` moved out of `digits`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Backspace with an empty inch field restores the feet digits into the
      inch field and clears `feetPart`.
- [x] Unit test.

---

## Resolution

`TradeCalcEngine.backspace()` with an empty inch field moves the feet value
back into the inch digits instead of discarding it. Test:
`TradeCalcTests.backspaceUndoesFeetKey`.

Verified: unit suite green on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 26.5); device re-check on the iPhone 13 Pro recorded below.
