# BUG-0001 — Bolt-up sequence diagram is not a star pattern

> Status: Resolved · Severity: High (wrong trade guidance) · Found: device QA 2026-09-03 (iPhone 13 Pro, iOS 26.6.1)

## Summary

The bolt-pattern diagram on the Bolt-Up Procedure sheet numbers bolts in
adjacent opposite pairs (1-2 at 0°/180°, then 3-4 at 45°/225°, …) instead
of the star / criss-cross sequence. Step 6 of the very same sheet says
"0°-180°, 90°-270°, 45°-225°, 135°-315°", so the diagram contradicts the
procedure text it sits above.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Modules → Flanges, Class 2500, 2" (8 bolts) → tap **Procedure**.
2. Read the numbers around the circle clockwise from the top.

## Expected vs Actual

- **Expected (8 bolts, clockwise from top):** 1 · 5 · 3 · 7 · 2 · 6 · 4 · 8
  (bolts 3/4 at 90°/270°, then 5/6 at 45°/225°).
- **Actual:** 1 · 3 · 5 · 7 · 2 · 4 · 6 · 8 — bolt 3 sits 45° from bolt 1.
- 12 bolts (Class 2500, 12"): expected 1 · 5 · 9 · 3 · 7 · 11 · 2 · 6 · 10 · 4 · 8 · 12;
  actual 1 · 3 · 5 · 7 · 9 · 11 · 2 · 4 · 6 · 8 · 10 · 12.

## Root Cause

`BoltPatternDiagram.sequence` in `Views/BoltProcedureSheet.swift` walks
`current = remaining.min()` after each opposite pair, which is simply the
next adjacent hole, never the quarter-point.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Sequence is the quarter-based star for every multiple-of-4 bolt count
      (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 44): pairs (k, k+n/2), then
      (k+n/4, k+3n/4), for k = 0 ..< n/4.
- [x] Unit test locks 4-, 8-, 12-, 16-, 20- and 28-bolt sequences.
- [x] Non-multiple-of-4 counts still produce a valid permutation.

---

## Resolution

Replaced the walk-to-next-adjacent loop with `BoltSequence` in
[`Views/BoltProcedureSheet.swift`](../../PipingDatabase/Views/BoltProcedureSheet.swift):
for n = 4k bolts the order is (k, k+n/2, k+n/4, k+3n/4) for each k, which is
the star pattern step 6 describes (8 bolts read 1·5·3·7·2·6·4·8 clockwise;
12 bolts 1·5·9·3·7·11·2·6·10·4·8·12). Non-multiples of four fall back to
opposite pairs stepping a quarter turn. `BugRegressionTests.starSequence`
locks 4/8/12/16/20 and `everyFlangeBoltCountIsAValidStar` checks every bolt
count in Flanges.json is a permutation of opposite pairs.

Verified: 60 unit + 5 UI tests green on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 26.5), and re-checked on the iPhone 13 Pro (iOS 26.6.1) via WebDriverAgent on 2026-09-03 — the reproduction steps above now produce the expected result.
