# Privacy Policy — PipeBase

**Effective date:** August 29, 2026

PipeBase is an offline engineering reference application.

## Data collection

PipeBase does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any
personal data. The app:

- makes **no network requests** of any kind;
- contains **no analytics, tracking, or advertising** frameworks;
- requires **no account, sign-in, or registration**;
- requests **no device permissions** (no location, camera, contacts,
  microphone, or photos).

## Data stored on your device

Your preferences (unit system, display options, module order) and your
favorites/recent lookups are stored locally on your device only, using
Apple's standard storage frameworks. This data never leaves your device
and is removed when you delete the app.

## Sharing features

When you use the Share Datasheet feature, the text you share goes only
to the destination you choose through Apple's standard share sheet. The
app itself transmits nothing.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an
online feature), the updated policy will be posted at this address
before the change takes effect.
